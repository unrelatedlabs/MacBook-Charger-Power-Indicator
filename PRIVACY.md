# Privacy Policy

**MacBook Charger Power Indicator**
Effective date: June 6, 2026

## Summary

MacBook Charger Power Indicator does **not collect, store, transmit, or share
any personal data**. Everything the app shows is read locally from macOS and
stays on your Mac.

## What the app accesses

The app reads your Mac's power information locally through Apple's IOKit
frameworks — battery charge, health, cycle count, temperature, voltage and
current, and details about the connected power adapter (wattage, voltage,
current, and USB-C power-delivery profiles).

This information is:

- **Read only** — the app never modifies your battery or hardware settings.
- **Displayed only** — it is shown in the menu bar and dropdown and is never
  recorded to disk, logged, or sent anywhere.
- **Never transmitted** — the app has **no network access** of any kind.

## Data collection

- No personal data is collected.
- No usage analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising or tracking identifiers.
- No third-party SDKs or services.
- No accounts, and no data leaves your device.

## Login item

If you enable "Start at Login," the app registers itself with macOS using the
system `SMAppService` API so it can launch automatically. This is handled
entirely by macOS; no information is shared with the developer.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
