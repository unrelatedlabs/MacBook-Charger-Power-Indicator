## Description  [≤4000 chars]
See exactly how much power your MacBook is drawing from its charger — right in the menu bar.

This app replaces the usual battery icon with a clear, compact readout: your battery percentage when you're unplugged, and the live charging wattage (like 86W) the moment you connect a charger. One glance tells you whether you're getting full speed from your adapter and cable.

Click the icon for a detailed breakdown:

• Battery — charge %, condition, health, cycle count, capacity, temperature, voltage and current
• Power Adapter — name, wattage, negotiated voltage/current, manufacturer, model, serial and firmware
• USB-C Power Profiles — every voltage/current level your charger and cable negotiate

Why you'll like it:

• Instantly spot an underpowered charger (e.g. a weak USB port delivering only a few watts)
• Compare chargers and cables by the wattage they actually deliver
• Keep an eye on long-term battery health and cycle count
• Lightweight and fully native — no background services

Privacy: everything is read locally from macOS. The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no network access at all.

Turn on "Start at Login" with a single toggle in the menu, and it quietly lives in your menu bar — no Dock icon, no windows.
